Same API. Same risk.
Three threat models.

Cookies, localStorage, and sessionStorage all look like a key-value bag — but one ships with the request, one survives forever, one dies with the tab. Pick wrong and you ship XSS or CSRF.

🍪 Cookie

💾 localStorage

never store JWTs here
🪟 sessionStorage
